Ground Yourself

Follow a short grounding exercise to reconnect with your surroundings while you decide what support you need.

GROUNDING EXERCISE

5–4–3–2–1 Grounding

Move through these prompts slowly. There is no need to rush.

5

Notice 5 things you can see

Look around and quietly identify five things in your surroundings.

4

Notice 4 things you can feel

Notice physical sensations such as your feet on the floor or clothing against your skin.

3

Notice 3 things you can hear

Pay attention to sounds near you and farther away.

2

Notice 2 things you can smell

Notice any scents around you. If you cannot smell anything, think of two familiar scents.

1

Notice 1 thing you can taste

Notice a taste in your mouth or take a sip of water if available.
